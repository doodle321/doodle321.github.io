Our Story

Three generations,
one degchi.

What started as a small dhaba near the old bus stand in 1998 is today one of Hyderabad's most loved family restaurants. The recipes haven't changed — neither has the love.

🔥

Charcoal-fired BBQ

Seekh kebabs grilled fresh on open angithi, the Hyderabadi way.

🌾

Local Sindhi ingredients

Basmati from Larkana, spices ground daily in-house.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family hall & takeaway

Comfortable family seating, quick takeaway counter, and home delivery.
