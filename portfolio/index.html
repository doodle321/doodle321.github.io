About

I'm a developer and social media strategist based in Hyderabad, Sindh. My work centers on building complete, functional web experiences — from interface to underlying logic — using clean, maintainable code.

Alongside development, I work on the strategy side of digital presence: planning content, structuring growth, and shaping how a brand shows up and engages with its audience online.

I approach both disciplines the same way — start with the goal, keep the execution disciplined, and avoid anything that doesn't earn its place.
