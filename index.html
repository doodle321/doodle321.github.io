FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers to the things people usually ask before starting a project.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the scope — a landing page costs less than a full restaurant site with ordering. Every project gets a clear, fixed quote up front, so there are no surprises. Message me with what you need and I will send a detailed estimate.

How long does a project take?

Most single-page sites are ready within 5–7 days, and full multi-section websites typically take 2–3 weeks. I give you a timeline before work starts and share progress updates as each stage is completed.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. Every project includes a round of revisions after the first full draft, and small tweaks are always quick and easy to arrange. My goal is a site you are completely happy to put your name on.

Can my customers order through WhatsApp?

Absolutely. I build cart and checkout flows that generate a complete order message and open WhatsApp with everything pre-filled — no apps or accounts needed for your customers.

What happens after the site launches?

You get post-launch support for fixes and small updates, plus guidance if you want to manage content yourself. Longer-term maintenance and social media support are also available.
