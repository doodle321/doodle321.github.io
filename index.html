Common
questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope — a landing page costs less than a full restaurant site with ordering. Every project gets a clear, fixed quote up front. Message me with what you need and I'll send a detailed estimate.

How long does a project take?

Most single-page sites are ready within 5–7 days; full multi-section websites typically take 2–3 weeks. You get a timeline before work starts and progress updates at each stage.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. Every project includes a round of revisions after the first full draft, and small tweaks are always quick to arrange. The goal is a site you're completely happy to put your name on.

Can my customers order through WhatsApp?

Absolutely. I build cart and checkout flows that generate a complete order message and open WhatsApp with everything pre-filled — no apps or accounts needed for your customers.

What happens after the site launches?

You get post-launch support for fixes and small updates, plus guidance if you want to manage content yourself. Longer-term maintenance and social media support are also available.
