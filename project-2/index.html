Our Story

Three generations,
one degchi.

What started as a small dhaba near the old bus stand in 1998 is today one of Hyderabad's most loved family restaurants. No apps, no sign-ups — just add to cart, send your order on WhatsApp, and hot food reaches your doorstep.
