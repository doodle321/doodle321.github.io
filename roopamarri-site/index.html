Our Story

A family table,
grown into a celebration ground

What began as a roadside dhaba serving karahi to travellers on Waddhu Wah Road is today one of the region's most-loved family destinations. The recipes haven't changed — desi ghee, whole spices, slow fire — but the table has grown long enough to seat five hundred.

Whether you're here for a quiet Tuesday dinner or your daughter's walima, you're family the moment you walk in. That's the whole idea.

  • Cooked on open fire. Karahi and BBQ made fresh to order, never held under a lamp.
  • Honest ingredients. Farm produce daily, halal certified, prices on the menu — no surprises.
  • Room for everyone. Family halls, a rooftop terrace, and a floodlit marriage garden.
  • Celebrations done right. Decor, lights, catering and parking — one call, one team.
